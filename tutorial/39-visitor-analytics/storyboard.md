# Read visitor trends and filters — storyboard

Status: authored; audio and recording review pending.

## 01. Find the workflow

Visitors summarizes recorded site activity. Open Visitors and select the period you want to inspect. Read each metric according to its label: visits, unique visitors and returning visitors describe different measurements.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/visitors`.
- Show and check **Visitors**.

## 02. Understand the controls

Use the available filters to separate human and bot activity and compare periods where supported. Check the active dates before interpreting a rise or fall. A filtered result is not necessarily the total traffic for the shop.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/visitors`.
- Show and check **Visitors**.
- Show and check **Date from**.

## 03. Perform the task

Inspect the trend chart and breakdowns together. Traffic counts do not prove revenue, successful checkout or delivery. Use order records for those outcomes, and avoid presenting a visitor count as a conversion rate without the correct denominator.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/visitors`.
- Show and check **Visitors**.
- Show and check **Segment**.
- Show and check **Visitor type**.

## 04. Verify and continue

Treat analytics as a guide for investigation. Missing events, privacy choices and classification rules can affect the recorded data. Keep personal details private when showing reports and state the actual period when sharing a finding.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/visitors`.
- Show and check **Visitors**.
