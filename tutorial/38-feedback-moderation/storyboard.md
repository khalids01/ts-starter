# Review and moderate submitted feedback — storyboard

Status: authored; audio and recording review pending.

## 01. Find the workflow

Feedback collects submitted messages that need operator attention. Open Feedback and identify the message you intend to review. Read the actual content and context before changing its state.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/feedback`.
- Show and check **Feedback**.

## 02. Understand the controls

Use the available status controls to represent what your team has done. A status should not imply that the customer received a reply unless that communication actually happened. Keep private contact details out of shared recordings.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/feedback`.
- Show and check **{{feedbackText}}**.

## 03. Perform the task

Review the updated item and use pagination to find older submissions. Handle abusive or irrelevant content according to your store's policy. Do not remove a valid report simply because it describes an inconvenient problem.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/feedback`.
- Show and check **{{feedbackText}}**.

## 04. Verify and continue

For an order issue, use the order and customer workflows to investigate the underlying facts. Feedback moderation does not change inventory, issue a refund or resolve a shipment. Record operational actions in their proper sections.

Screen actions:

- Open `/admin/feedback`.
- Show and check **Feedback**.
